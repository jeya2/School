About the School

A neighbourhood school with a state-wide standard

A Tamil Nadu State Board school teaching Std I to Std XII across Tamil and English medium sections, with a fully digitised office — admissions, attendance, examinations and fees kept in one place.

Admissions 2026 – 27

Five steps, mostly online

Applications for Std I, Std VI and Std XI are open now. Lateral entry into other standards is subject to seat availability.

1 · Submit the enquiry

Fill the online form or visit the office between 9 AM and 3 PM on any working day.

2 · Document verification

Birth certificate, Aadhaar, community & nativity certificate, and the previous school TC.

3 · Interaction

A short, informal conversation with the child and parents. No written entrance test for Std I–VIII.

4 · Seat allotment

Allotment intimated by SMS. RTE 25% seats allotted per Government of Tamil Nadu norms.

5 · Fee payment & joining

Pay online or at the counter. Admission number and EMIS entry generated the same day.
